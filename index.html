01 / Research

From climate states
to climate transitions.

The Earth system can occupy different climate states under similar external forcing. My research asks why these states respond differently to the same perturbation — and what their stability can tell us about future climate change.

I combine coupled climate modelling, nonlinear dynamical-systems theory, climate networks and paleoclimate perspectives to investigate the emergence, stability and transitions of Earth's multiple equilibrium states.
